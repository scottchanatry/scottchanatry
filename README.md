# Hi, I'm Scott Chanatry 🖍️

I'm a **child psychiatrist** by training — currently on the longest rotation there is: **parenthood**. Stanford, then NYU School of Medicine, then psychiatry residency, then the two best teachers I've ever had.

Much of my working life was spent doing the slowest, most valuable thing there is: listening to kids, and to the families around them, until the picture makes sense. For now I've stepped away from clinical practice to be home with my own children while they're young.

Whatever's left over goes to building small software for the fun of it — a habit that started at Stanford and never really stopped.

## 🛠️ What I'm building

Lately the vibecoding has meant small tools that solve one household-sized problem at a time.

- **[scottchanatry.com](https://scottchanatry.com)** ([source](https://github.com/scottchanatry/scottchanatry.com)): my personal site. Hand-typed HTML, CSS, and ~120 lines of vanilla JavaScript, with no frameworks, no build step, and no trackers. There's a crayon button. You can draw on the page. Really.
- **[SpeakEasy](https://github.com/scottchanatry/SpeakEasyComplete)**: an iOS app written in Swift. Work in progress.

I like software that is small, readable, and does one job well.
