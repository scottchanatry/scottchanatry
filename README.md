# Scott Chanatry

**Child psychiatrist, Stanford. After years of listening to children, I now listen to my own.**

I'm Scott Chanatry. Much of my working life was spent doing the slowest, most valuable thing there is: listening to kids, and to the families around them, until the picture makes sense. For now I'm home with my own children while they're young, learning what training couldn't teach me.

Writing fiction and coding keep me busy the rest of the time. There are two books so far, *Behold Your Mother* and *Nobody Sent Me*, the first novels of the Aspartame series. The coding is small software for the fun of it, a habit that started at Stanford and never really stopped.

Everything lives at **[scottchanatry.com](https://scottchanatry.com)**.
